# CareLedger visual system

Bright minimal hospital workspace, as requested. Use DM Sans for functional text and Instrument Serif for the quieter welcome accent. Deep forest green is reserved for actions and positive emphasis. The operational workspace uses a 232px white sidebar, pale gray canvas, fine gray dividers, white panels, readable tables, and a clear appointment agenda. Violet and amber distinguish workflow states without carrying meaning alone.

The primary interaction is the daily clinical agenda beside a longitudinal record stream. Medical record verification appears as a compact seal and a detailed cryptographic receipt on demand. The network explorer uses a large white diagram canvas with green anchored blocks and labeled connections. No fictional validators or telemetry. All layouts collapse to one column on narrow screens. Motion respects reduced-motion preference.
